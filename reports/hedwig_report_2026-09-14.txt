## Cybersecurity
**IBM Security Forecasts Growing Infostealer Risks from AI Agents**
IBM's 2026 threat trends report identifies the growing use of AI chatbots and agents in business operations as a new attack surface for infostealer malware. Compromised systems containing stored chatbot credentials are flagged as an emerging risk.
Why you should care: For a Diploma IT student, this is a concrete warning about how your future work environments will face threats that didn't exist a few years ago. Understanding infostealer risks and how AI agent credentials are targeted gives you practical knowledge for securing any system you work with.
Impact: High
Source: IBM

## India Policy
**India's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 Operationalize DPDPA**
On 13 November 2025, MeitY notified the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025, following a 10-month wait since the draft Rules were released on 3 January 2025. These Rules operationalize the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA), India's first comprehensive data protection law.
Why you should care: As a developing IT professional in India, you will inevitably work with systems that handle user data. These rules define your obligations around data consent, breach notification, and data principal rights. Understanding them is essential for compliance in any role involving user data.
Impact: High
Source: HLC
