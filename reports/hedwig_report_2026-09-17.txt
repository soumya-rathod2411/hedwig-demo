## Cybersecurity

**ClickFix attack trends** (Huntress, 2 Oct 2025; ZDNet, 17 Oct 2025)
What happened: ClickFix remains a widely adopted initial attack technique since early 2024, tricking users into copying and pasting commands into the Run dialog box, which executes malicious code. Microsoft has tracked its increase in popularity over the past year.
Why you should care: This is a social-engineering attack vector you may encounter in real jobs. Understanding how "fix this broken CAPTCHA" scams work helps you recognize and avoid them.
Impact: Medium
Source: Huntress, ZDNet

**AI chatbot attack surface** (IBM, 11 Mar 2026)
What happened: IBM forecasts that the growing use of AI chatbots and agents in business operations creates a new attack surface for infostealer malware. Compromised AI agents containing stored chatbot credentials are an emerging risk.
Why you should care: As AI integration becomes standard in business software, security incidents will increasingly involve AI agents. Knowing this trend helps you design more secure integrations.
Impact: Medium
Source: IBM
