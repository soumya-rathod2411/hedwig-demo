## 3 Things I Absolutely Should Know Today

**1. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 fully operationalize the DPDPA -- compliance timeline is now defined**
- The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) notified the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 on 13 November 2025, fully operationalizing India's first comprehensive data protection law. The compliance regime is now taking shape with defined timelines for Consent Manager requirements and other provisions. Businesses and developers building applications handling user data in India must align with these Rules, which were notified after a 10-month wait since the draft was released on 3 January 2025.
- **Why you should care**: As a Diploma IT student in India, you will likely build or work on applications that handle user data. Understanding the DPDPA and its implementing Rules is essential for compliance -- this isn't just a legal formality; it affects how you design data collection, storage, and user consent mechanisms in any project involving Indian users.
- **Impact**: High
- **What to do**: Review the DPDPA Rules 2025 requirements, especially around Consent Managers and data principal rights. If building applications, audit your data flows against these principles now rather than later.
- **Source**: Stephenson Harwood; iclg.com; McDermott Law

## India Policy

**Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025 fully operationalize DPDPA**
- On 13 November 2025, India's Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) notified the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules 2025, following a 10-month wait since the draft Rules were released on 3 January 2025. These Rules operationalize the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA), India's first comprehensive data protection law. The Rules will be implemented in phases over the next 12 to 18 months through a series of notifications. The compliance regime is now taking shape with defined timelines for Consent Manager requirements and other provisions.
- **Why you should care**: As a Diploma IT student in India, you will likely build or work on applications that handle user data. Understanding the DPDPA and its implementing Rules is essential for compliance -- this affects how you design data collection, storage, and user consent mechanisms in any project involving Indian users.
